# Frontend Design (Anthropic Official Skill)

Approach this as the design lead at a studio known for giving every project a distinctive visual identity that could not be mistaken for anyone else's. Avoid templated proposals and defaults: make deliberate, opinionated choices about palette, typography, and layout that are specific to this brief.

## 1. Ground It in the Subject
- Pin down the subject's world, its materials, instruments, artifacts, and vernacular.
- For Progress Quest, the subject is **classic zero-player RPGs, retro operating systems, and stat-dense dashboards**.

## 2. Design Principles
- **Hero & Headline**: Open with the most characteristic element of the subject's world.
- **Typography & Personality**: Pair display and body faces deliberately. Set a clear type scale with intentional weights, widths, and spacing.
- **Structure is Information**: Use structural devices (eyebrows, badges, progress bars, cards, dividers) to encode real content, not decorate.
- **Motion & Atmosphere**: Leverage subtle micro-animations (shimmer sweeps, smooth bar fill transitions, active glows).
- **Match Vision to Complexity**: For stat dashboards, precision in alignment, spacing, and micro-hierarchy creates elegance.

## 3. Process: Brainstorm, Explore, Plan, Build, Critique
- Avoid AI defaults (cream background with terracotta, plain black with acid green, or generic unstyled cards).
- Use curated OKLCH color space palettes and custom CSS design tokens.
